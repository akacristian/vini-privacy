Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Vini (“the app”) is a wine education app for iOS, operated by Cristian Cruz (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what control you have over it.

The short version: we store your learning progress so you don’t lose it, we collect crash reports so we can fix bugs, and we don’t sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Information we collect

Learning progress (synced to our servers)

When you use the app, we store your learning progress — completed lessons, experience points (XP), streak count, and the date of your last lesson — in our cloud database so it survives reinstalls and can follow you across devices. This data is tied to a randomly generated account identifier that is created silently the first time you launch the app. Until you sign in, this identifier is anonymous and not connected to your name or email.

Account information (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional. If you choose “Sign in with Apple” or “Continue with Google” to back up your progress, we receive from that provider:

  • a unique account identifier,
  • your name (if you share it), and
  • your email address (with Sign in with Apple you may use Apple’s private relay address instead of your real one).

We use this only to let you access the same progress on another device or after a reinstall. We do not send marketing email.

Crash and diagnostic data

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and non-fatal error reports (for example, when progress sync fails). These reports include device model, operating system version, and technical traces of what the app was doing at the time. They are not used for advertising and are automatically deleted by our provider after a retention period (currently 90 days).

Information that stays on your device

Some information never leaves your iPhone:

  • your onboarding answers (your first name, your email address if you chose to give it, your daily goal and preferred study time, why you want to learn about wine, and what makes wine feel intimidating),
  • your notification settings and scheduled streak reminders (reminders are local notifications — no server is involved).

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not sell anything in the app. Vini is free, with no purchases or subscriptions.
  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or health data.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children. Vini is an educational app about wine and is intended for users of legal drinking age in their country.

Who processes the data

Our backend is Google Firebase (Google LLC): Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (progress storage), and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting). Google processes this data on our behalf; their handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase’s data processing terms. If you sign in with Google, Google’s own sign-in service also applies.

How long we keep it

Your progress and account data are kept until you delete your account. Crash reports are retained by our provider for their standard retention period and then deleted automatically.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all synced progress from our servers and resets the app on your device. It cannot be undone.

If you can’t access the app, you can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. The in-app deletion covers most of this; for anything else, contact us and we’ll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date at the top and, for significant changes, note it in the app. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
