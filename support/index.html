Common questions

Is Vini really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no purchases, and no subscriptions. Every lesson is available to everyone; lessons simply unlock one at a time as you work through the course.

How do I keep my progress if I change phones?

Open Profile → Save your progress and sign in with Apple or Google. Your streak, XP, and completed lessons are then backed up and come back when you sign in on another iPhone.

How do I switch between English and Spanish?

Open Profile → Settings → Language. This takes you to iOS Settings, where you can choose English or Español. Vini restarts in the language you pick.

I’m not getting my daily reminder.

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Vini → Notifications, and choose a study time in Profile → Settings → Study time. On days you’ve already done a lesson, Vini skips the reminder.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and synced progress from our servers. If you can’t open the app, email us and we’ll delete it for you.

Who is Vini for?

Anyone curious about wine who is of legal drinking age where they live. No prior knowledge needed: every term is explained before it’s used.
